Quick answers {{ neo_ico('neutral', 58, 'lime') }}

Short answers to what people ask most.

How do I book a service?

Find a workshop, choose the service you need and send a booking request for the time you want. The workshop confirms it, and you can follow its status under My Bookings.

How do I know if a workshop is open?

Every workshop shows an Open or Closed badge in the search results and on its own page. You can still send a request to a closed workshop for a later time.

I forgot my password.

Choose "Forgot password?" in the login window and we will email you a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Google, you can simply keep using "Sign in with Google".

I own a workshop. How do I list it?

Sign up as a Workshop Owner, then add your workshop, its services and your opening status from the owner dashboard. Customers will find you in search.

I am a mechanic. How do I join a workshop?

Sign up as a Mechanic, browse workshops and send a join request. The owner reviews it, and once you are in you can mark yourself active or absent so the owner knows who is available.
