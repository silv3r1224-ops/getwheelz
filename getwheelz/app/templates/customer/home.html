{# ===================== VEHICLE CARE TIPS ===================== #}

Vehicle care tips

Small habits that save big repairs.

Check tire pressure

Look at it monthly and before long trips. Correct pressure improves grip and tire life.

Change oil on schedule

Follow the interval in your owner's manual. Old oil wears the engine faster.

Do not ignore warning lights

A light on the dashboard is an early warning. Getting it checked is cheaper than a breakdown.

Service the AC before summer

If the cooling feels weak, get it looked at before the hottest weeks arrive.

{# ===================== FAQ ===================== #}

Good to know

Quick answers about bookings and distances.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

A new request starts as Pending. When the workshop accepts it, the status changes to Confirmed, and after the visit it becomes Completed. You can follow all of this under My Bookings.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. While a booking is still Pending you can cancel it from My Bookings.

How is the distance worked out?

It is the straight-line distance from your device's location to the workshop's pin on the map, so the road distance may be a little longer. Your location stays in your browser and is not saved.

Why can't I see distances?

Allow location access when your browser asks. If you blocked it earlier, turn it back on in the site settings of your browser, then use the Enable button on this page.
