Privacy Policy

Version {{ version }} · Getwheelz (demo platform)

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, role, an encrypted (hashed) password, and an optional profile picture. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and verified email from Google.
  • Bookings: the workshop, service, requested time, status, the vehicle type and the vehicle description you type, and the bill and payment status.
  • Payments: the amount, method, a masked reference (for example the last digits of a card or a UPI id) and a receipt number. At this stage payment is a demonstration and no real money moves.
  • Reviews: your rating and text. Owners' public replies are stored with them.
  • Reports and appeals: what you write when you report someone, respond to a report, or appeal a decision, and the admin's notes.
  • Workshops and owners: workshop name, address, map position, phone and WhatsApp number, photos and services.
  • Consent record: which version of these documents you accepted, and when.
  • Technical: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a security (CSRF) token. We use your browser's location only to show distances; it is sent with your search and is not saved to your profile.

2. Why we use it

To run accounts, show workshops and distances, handle bookings and payments, show ratings, keep the platform safe, review reports and appeals, and tell you what is happening through your inbox.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone, even without an account: approved workshops, their services, ratings, public reviews (with first name and last initial) and owners' public replies.
  • signed-in customers and mechanics: a workshop's phone and WhatsApp number, through the Call and WhatsApp buttons. Visitors who are not signed in are asked to log in instead and never see the number.
  • A workshop owner and the assigned mechanic: the details of bookings at their workshop, including the customer's name, the service and the vehicle description.
  • The customer: their own bookings, the mechanic assigned and the status.
  • Admins: account details, bookings, reports, appeals and consent records, as needed to run and protect the platform. A person who is reported is never told who reported them.

4. Notifications

We use the Getwheelz inbox. We do not send notification emails for now. The only email we send is a password-reset link when you ask for one.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only as described above, with Google if you choose Google sign-in, and when the law requires it. A future payment provider would process payments under its own policy.

6. Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account exists.
