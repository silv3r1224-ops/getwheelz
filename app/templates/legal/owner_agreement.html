Workshop Owner Agreement

Version {{ version }} · Getwheelz (demo platform)

This agreement is in addition to the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy. You accept it before you list a workshop.

1. Your listing

  • What you enter about your workshop (name, address, photos, services, vehicle types, phone and WhatsApp number) must be true and up to date.
  • You choose whether you serve four-wheelers, two-wheelers or both, and offer only services from the matching catalogs.
  • Your listing stays hidden until an admin approves it. If it is rejected you will see the reason; you can fix it and resubmit, or appeal once per rejection.

2. Bookings

  • Accept or decline each new booking. A request you do not answer within 48 hours is cancelled automatically, and you are reminded before that.
  • After a customer's booking is accepted they can only ask to cancel; you decide on their request.
  • After accepting, assign one of your mechanics, and move the job through In progress and Completed honestly.
  • The customer pays after the job is completed. Set the bill accurately.

3. Customers and mechanics

Treat customers and your mechanics fairly. Customers' details are for dealing with their booking only. Your phone and WhatsApp number are shown to signed-in customers and mechanics so they can reach you; do not use theirs for anything else.

4. Reviews

You may post one public reply to each review of your workshop. Replies must be honest and respectful. You may not ask customers to remove reviews.

5. Reports and suspension

Reports about your workshop or account are reviewed. You may be asked to respond within 72 hours. A verified report can lead to a warning or, for accounts, a suspension; you can appeal once per decision.

6. Changes

If we change this agreement in a way that matters, we publish a new version and ask you to accept it again. We record which version you accepted and when.
