Our mission

Finding a reliable mechanic shouldn't mean asking around for recommendations or gambling on the nearest garage. Getwheelz keeps finding and booking a mechanic simple — search by the service you need, see real workshops near you, and book directly without the back-and-forth.

{# ===================== WHAT WE OFFER ===================== #}
What we offer

One platform, four ways to care.

Tools for drivers, workshop owners and mechanics — everything in one calm place.

Find workshops

Search by service type and location to discover mechanics near you.

Book online

Send a booking request through the platform and follow its status — no phone calls needed.

For workshop owners

List your services, show customers when you are open, and manage booking requests in one place.

For mechanics

Join a workshop that fits your skills and let owners know when you are active for work.
