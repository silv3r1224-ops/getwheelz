Questions, answered

How do I book a service?

Search for a workshop or browse by service, open its page, choose a service and a time, and send the request. The workshop confirms it and you can follow its status under My Bookings.

Do I need an account?

You can search and look at workshops without one. You need a free account to book, and it only takes a minute to create.

Which services can I find?

Engine repair, electrical work, tires, AC service and general servicing, plus anything else workshops add to their listing.

How do mechanics join a workshop?

A mechanic sends a joining request from a workshop's page. The owner reviews it and accepts it if the workshop has an open spot.

Can a mechanic leave a workshop?

Yes. They send a leave request and the owner decides, so the team is never left short without notice.
